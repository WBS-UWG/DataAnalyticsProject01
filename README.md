# 1. Problem statements
The datasource I decided to use is the GCAT (General Catalog of Artificial Space Objects) dataset, which contains information about various artificial satellites and space objects.
The link to the dataset is: [GCAT Dataset](https://planet4589.org/space/gcat/root.html)

## Dataset overview :
- size => contains +31K records of artificial space objects.
- columns => includes information such as object name, type, launch date, and orbital parameters.
- Recent data => The last update of the dataset was on 2026 sep 8

## Main Inquiry Question
    How has global space launch activity evolved since 1957, and which nations and rocket technologies have dominated space access over time?

Understanding the expansion of artificial space objects requires looking beyond raw mission counts to examine the underlying geopolitical, technological, and economic forces driving space access. By evaluating launch frequency, vehicle hardware selection, and national ownership patterns, this investigation breaks down the overarching inquiry into three core sub-questions structured around specific data distributions and visualization techniques:

### Sub-question 1: Annual Launch Frequency during history

How has the annual volume of global space launches evolved from 1957 to the present day.
Maybe we can also see how historical events and technological advancement have influenced that frequency


### Sub-question 2: Vehicle Utilization Share

Which specific launch vehicle models have been most frequently deployed throughout spaceflight history, and what proportion of total orbital missions does each primary rocket family represent?

### Sub-question 3: Geopolitical Market Share

Which operating nation holds the largest cumulative share of space deployments, and how is global space access partitioned among leading spacefaring nations?
